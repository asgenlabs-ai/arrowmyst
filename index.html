Effective: 11 October 2026

Gameplay and local saves

Arrow Myst includes arrow puzzles, difficulty practice and daily challenges. No account is required. Levels work offline. Progress, hearts, hint usage, completion dates, elapsed play time, points balances, reward-claim markers, ad-free access and expiry, sound/haptics preferences, the accepted Terms version, reminder preferences and reminder timing remain in Android's private app storage. ASGEN LABS does not operate a gameplay server or receive those saves. Cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled.

Advertising and third-party services

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. Banners appear only in gameplay. Three hints per level are free; optional rewarded ads offer extra hints, one-life continues, restarts and 2× completion points; occasional interstitials can appear between levels. Ads require Internet access and are unavailable when offline. There are no real-money purchases. You can confirm point-based ad-free access in Settings; active access disables every ad format. Disabling ads does not erase information previously processed by Google.

Google's advertising services can collect and share IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), device/advertising identifiers, ad interactions, app usage related to ads, and diagnostics such as crash or performance information. Google uses these data for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Data handling depends on consent, platform settings, region and advertising configuration. Google may process information in other countries and retains it under its own policies. Ads, including test ads, may process data; this is not a no-data-collection app. Arrow Myst does not request precise location, contacts, microphone, camera or external-storage access.

Google's consent service checks privacy requirements when the app opens after Terms acceptance. Where a consent message is required, it presents choices before ads are requested. Where required, Settings includes Ad privacy choices to review those choices. If advertising eligibility cannot be established, gameplay remains available and ads are not requested. You can also manage advertising preferences in Android and Google account settings. Accepting our Terms is separate from advertising consent and does not enable reminders.

Android permissions

Internet (INTERNET) and network status (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) support online ads and consent checks. Google advertising permissions (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) support advertising identifiers, attribution and advertising topics where permitted by Android and your choices; they do not provide precise GPS location.

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13 and later) are requested only when you choose to enable Gameplay reminders in Settings. Denying permission or leaving reminders off does not limit gameplay. Android background-work permissions (WAKE_LOCK and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) allow short, deferrable local work and restoration of scheduled work after a device restart. The app does not use exact alarms or a foreground service for reminders. A signature-protected internal receiver permission prevents other apps from sending untrusted internal broadcasts; it grants no access to your personal information. These normal or internal permissions are not separate prompts for personal-data access.

Optional gameplay reminders

Reminders are off by default. When enabled, the app can send one local reminder after at least 4 hours away from the game, at most once every 24 hours, between 8 a.m. and 10 p.m. local time. Android battery and background restrictions may delay or prevent delivery. Opening the app cancels the pending reminder. The reminder may show your completed-level count, contains no ads and plays no sound. Progress is hidden in the public lock-screen version. Turn reminders off in Settings or use Android notification controls. Reminder content and scheduling stay on your device; no push-notification server or push token is used.

Your choices and deletion

Settings → Reset all progress deletes saved gameplay and points and cancels a pending reminder. It preserves purchased ad-free access, Terms acceptance, reminder choice and sound/haptics preferences. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local saves and preferences; there is no cloud recovery. This does not erase data already handled by Google; use Google's privacy controls for those data. Daily challenges use your device's local calendar date. There are no logins, chat, publisher-run analytics or user-generated content.

Support email

Support opens an external email app. ASGEN LABS receives information you voluntarily include, including your email address and issue description, and uses it to answer your request. The app does not automatically attach gameplay saves, identifiers or logs. Your email provider's policy applies. We retain correspondence only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact support@asgenlabs.com for access, correction or deletion requests, subject to applicable law and retention duties. Contact Google separately for data handled by its advertising services.

Using the game

Arrow Myst is provided by ASGEN LABS for personal entertainment. On first launch, read these Terms and choose Accept & continue to play. Gameplay and advertising

Version 1.0.0 includes 20,008 solvable puzzles based on 5,002 distinct shapes, four difficulty groups and daily challenges based on your device's date. Easy starts with 3 lives, Medium with 4, and Hard and Hardest with 5. A blocked arrow costs one life. When lives run out, an earned video reward adds one life on the same board, preserving cleared arrows, hint usage and elapsed play time. This can be repeated whenever lives run out; closing a video early or an unavailable video grants no life. A restart explicitly resets the board and restores the starting lives. Three free hints are available per level round. After those, each earned video reward grants one extra hint. Restarts retain the used free-hint count until a completed round is replayed. A hint highlights an arrow that currently has a clear exit.

Video rewards are granted only after the advertising provider confirms an earned reward; closing a video early does not grant a reward or consume a hint. If a video is unavailable, you can keep playing. Restarts have an explicit fallback when videos are unavailable. Ads can be videos, playable ads or other formats selected by the provider. Occasional interstitials appear only at level transitions. There are no accounts or real-money purchases.

Points and play time

For a completed round, base points equal the starting arrow count squared divided by elapsed foreground play seconds. Seconds round up, with a minimum of one second and at least one earned point. For example, 60 arrows in 60 seconds earn 60 points; 30 seconds earn 120; 10 seconds earn 360. Time pauses while the app is in the background or a reward video is being requested or shown, and stops when the round ends. Each restart begins a new timer. Completed rounds from earlier versions without recorded play time do not earn retroactive points.

Base points save automatically on completion. An earned completion video adds the same amount once more, for 2× points. Closing a video early grants no bonus. Reopening a completed round or receiving a duplicate callback cannot credit that round again; a fresh replay can earn new points. You can explicitly spend 500 points to skip one rewarded hint, retry or bonus video, or an available level-transition ad. This single skip does not remove banners. In Settings, choose and confirm ad-free access for 7 days (100,000 points), 30 days (1,000,000 points) or permanently (10,000,000 points). The selected cost is deducted only after confirmation; reaching a balance never activates access automatically. All ad formats are disabled during active access; hint, retry and one-time bonus rewards remain available without videos or additional point charges. Temporary purchases extend existing unexpired access and use the device clock. Points have no cash value, cannot be purchased for money or transferred, and stay on this device. Reset all progress clears points but keeps purchased ad-free access. Clearing app data or uninstalling deletes both points and ad-free access; there is no account or cloud recovery.

Saved progress

Saves remain on your device. Resetting progress, clearing storage or uninstalling deletes them. There is no cloud recovery service. Difficulty practice keeps your sequential journey's saved round separate. Completing a level counts only once. Updated puzzle layouts may reset an unfinished round while preserving completed levels.

Optional reminders

Gameplay reminders are off by default and can be enabled or disabled in Settings. When enabled and permitted by Android, one local reminder may arrive after at least 4 hours away, at most once every 24 hours, during daytime hours. It contains no ads and plays no sound. Delivery may be delayed or prevented by Android settings. Notification permission is optional and does not affect gameplay.
